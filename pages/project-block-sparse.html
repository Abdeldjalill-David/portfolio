Block-Sparse Featurizers

This research explores how structured sparsity can be used to capture meaningful visual concept manifolds while improving interpretability in learned representations.

The project sits at the intersection of computer vision, representation learning, and theory-driven machine learning, with an eye on understanding how models organize information internally.

Research direction

  • Sparsity as a structural inductive bias
  • Visual concept organization in learned manifolds
  • Interpretable representation learning
