SIA LAB

SIA LAB is a research and applied AI initiative focused on deep learning, generative models, and open tools that make advanced machine learning more accessible and useful in real workflows.

The lab brings together experimental research, product thinking, and education, with a strong emphasis on interpretability, visual learning, and practical deployment of modern AI systems.

Focus areas

  • Generative AI and creative systems
  • Computer vision and representation learning
  • Interpretability and sparsity-driven research
  • Open-source tooling for researchers and developers
