TUSS ArXiv Recommender

TUSS is a research recommendation tool designed to help users discover relevant papers through a natural language interface and lightweight filtering logic.

The goal is to reduce the friction of browsing large academic feeds and surface meaningful work that aligns with a person’s current interests or topic of exploration.

What it does

  • Indexes arXiv-like research material
  • Matches user intent through natural-language prompts
  • Highlights relevant papers with fewer search steps
