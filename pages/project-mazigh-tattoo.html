Amazigh Tattoo Generator

This creative generative project explores how cultural motifs can be reimagined through modern machine learning and visual synthesis techniques.

It combines artistic exploration with computational design, using AI as a tool to study, remix, and reinterpret symbolic forms in new visual languages.

Project themes

  • Cultural symbolism and generative art
  • Modern reinterpretation of traditional motifs
  • Creative experimentation with visual synthesis
