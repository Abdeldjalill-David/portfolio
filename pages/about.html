I am an independent deep learning researcher and AI educator. My work spans neural networks, generative models, and scalable learning systems — with a particular interest in where theory meets practical, generalizable systems.

Through writing, teaching, and mentoring, I have reached more than 1,000 students via Medium articles, Zoom workshops, and in-person seminars. I believe research should not only push boundaries, but illuminate paths for others.

What I Do

I lead research and product efforts at SIA LAB, contribute as a researcher at AI-Plans.com, and have served as Chief Research Scientist at Ryniant Sciences, an AI-powered platform for accelerated molecular discovery.

My core focus includes interpretability, computer vision, generative AI, and building open tools that help researchers and developers work more effectively — from arXiv recommenders to educational notebooks on modern language models.
